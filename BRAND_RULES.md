# 🎨 Social Skills — Official Brand System & Design Laws (v4.0)
**Site**: `socialskills.ninja`  
**Identity**: Autonomous Multi-Platform Auto-Poster & AI Cross-Publishing Engine  
**Aesthetic Theme**: Luxury Pixar 3D Plushy Lego (Light Blue-Grey Studio Base, Sapphire Obsidian Cards, Wu-Tang Yellow #ffc800, Azure Neon Cyan #00f0ff)

---

## 1. Brand Voice & Tone Architecture

### 🎙 Core Principles
1. **Brutally Honest & Relatable**: Tell it how it is. No corporate fluff or generic SaaS platitudes (*"Streamline your synergies"*). Speak directly to creators and engineers about real pain points.
2. **Authoritative & Engineering-Grade**: Frame the product as an enterprise-class cross-publishing framework built by social media engineers and AI architects.
3. **No Founder Persona Mentions**: Never mention "Jack" or individual founder names. The platform is an open, high-scale engine.
4. **Subtle Psychological Drivers**:
   - **Loss Aversion**: Highlight lost reach/impressions from manual cross-posting friction.
   - **Us vs. Them Contrast**: 1 unified desk vs. 10 chaotic browser tabs.
   - **Ego Validation & Status**: Empower creators with status signals ("Top 1% Shipping Frequency").
5. **SEO & GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)**: Every major heading and metadata block must weave in high-intent keywords for Google, Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Claude indexing.

## 2. Visual & Shader Design System

### 🌌 Color Tokens
- **Studio Backdrop**: `linear-gradient(180deg, #e2e8f0 0%, #cbd5e1 50%, #b0c2de 100%)` (Cool Light Blue-Grey Studio Slate — NOT stark white, NOT dark, rich blue-grey atmosphere).
- **Plush Surface Cards**: `#0f172a` (Sapphire Obsidian Dark Plush Container with 28px rounded corners and 3D bevel rims).
- **Primary Accent 1**: `#ffc800` (**Pure Wu-Tang Yellow**, `.btn-wutang`, `.plush-badge-wutang`, `.text-wutang-metallic`).
- **Primary Accent 2**: `#00f0ff` (**Electric Azure Neon Cyan**, `.btn-azure`, `.plush-badge-azure`, `.text-azure-metallic`).
- **Success Signal**: `#00f0ff` (Azure Neon Cyan) or `#ffc800` (Wu-Tang Yellow) — 0% emerald green slop.

### ✍️ Typography Laws
- **Headings on Light Studio Page Background (`h1, h2, h3, .heading-3d`)**:
  - **Scale**: XL (`text-4xl sm:text-6xl md:text-7xl font-black`)
  - **Alignment**: Centered (`text-center !important`)
  - **Color**: Deep Slate Charcoal (`#0f172a !important`)
  - **Clean Typography**: ZERO cheap black outline strokes (`-webkit-text-stroke: none`), ZERO dirty drop shadow smudges (`text-shadow: none`).
  - **Consistent Title Accents**: Main heading titles MUST consistently alternate between **Wu-Tang Yellow (`#ffc800` / `.text-wutang-metallic`)** and **Azure Neon Cyan (`#00f0ff` / `.text-azure-metallic`)** for highlighted title text!
- **Headings INSIDE Dark Plush Cards (`.plush-card h1, h2, h3`)**:
  - Pure White (`#ffffff`) with subtle 3D depth.
- **Non-Heading Body Text**:
  - MUST BE **DEEP SLATE / BLACK** (`#0f172a` / `#1e293b`) on light studio backgrounds to guarantee AAA contrast.
  - Inside dark plush cards (`#0f172a`), body text uses high-contrast light slate (`#f8fafc` / `#cbd5e1`).

### 🎛 Component Standards
- **Buttons**:
  - `.btn-wutang`: Pure Wu-Tang Yellow (`#ffc800`) 3D plush button with high-contrast black typography (`#000000`) and 2px white bevel rim.
  - `.btn-azure`: Electric Azure Neon Cyan (`#00f0ff`) 3D plush button with high-contrast black typography (`#000000`) and 2px white bevel rim.
  - `.btn-dark`: Sapphire Obsidian (`#0f172a`) 3D plush button with crisp white typography (`#ffffff`).
- **Input Fields**: Inset `#020617` dark enclosures, 2px border (`rgba(0,240,255,0.3)`), crisp white text, and custom cyan-glowing scrollbars (`.custom-scrollbar`).
- **Pill Badges**: Highlight text inside badges MUST use metallic yellow (`#ffc800`) or azure neon cyan (`#00f0ff`), NEVER white-on-white.
- **Bottom CTA Section**: Clean Sapphire Obsidian Plush Container (`#0f172a`) with crisp Wu-Tang Yellow & Azure Neon CTA buttons — ZERO cheap or murky background gradients.
- **Strict Rule**: **"NO EMOJIS"** — 0% unicode emojis in codebase text. Use CSS pills or SVG vector logos.
